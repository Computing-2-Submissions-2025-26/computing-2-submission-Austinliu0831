Cloud Elf Race

Game Rules

As night falls, the Red and Blue cloud elf teams — each with two brave racers — kick off a clockwise chase across the starry grid! You must roll a lucky 6 to wake a teammate from Home to start (which also grants you an extra turn), and the first player to loop around, navigate their colored final lane, and land exactly both pieces into the Finish wins! Oh, and if you happen to bump directly into an opponent, you'll blow them right back Home to sleep!
